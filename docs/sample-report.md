# Sample Report

> Question: *What production patterns are essential for shipping a Retrieval-Augmented Generation system?*

## Executive summary

Production RAG systems combine hybrid retrieval (BM25 plus dense vector
search, fused via Reciprocal Rank Fusion) with cross-encoder reranking to
get strong precision@5 on diverse queries. Equally important is making
faithfulness measurable: citation-enforced generation plus an
LLM-as-judge entailment check produces a per-claim faithfulness score.
Production deployments additionally need explicit evaluation (precision@k,
recall@k, MRR, faithfulness) with results attributable to specific
commits, and infrastructure choices (pgvector with HNSW, generated
tsvector columns) that keep retrieval correct even under concurrent
writes.

## Sub-question findings

### How should retrieval be structured?  ·  confidence 0.90

- **BM25 wins on rare terms; dense retrieval wins on paraphrase; Reciprocal Rank Fusion combines them without needing to calibrate score scales across rankers (canonical k=60).**
  - [A Survey of Retrieval-Augmented Generation Methods (2024)](https://example.com/rag-survey-2024)

- **Cross-encoder rerankers (e.g. ms-marco-MiniLM) on the top fused candidates typically deliver 5-15% precision@5 improvements at moderate latency cost.**
  - [A Survey of Retrieval-Augmented Generation Methods (2024)](https://example.com/rag-survey-2024)

### How should answers be grounded and measured?  ·  confidence 0.80

- **Faithfulness is the share of generated claims actually entailed by their cited sources, measured via an LLM-as-judge entailment check at temperature 0.**
  - [Measuring Faithfulness in RAG Answers](https://example.com/llm-eval-faithfulness)

- **RAGAS automates faithfulness alongside complementary metrics: context precision, context recall, and answer relevancy — reporting them together catches regressions that retrieval-only metrics miss.**
  - [Measuring Faithfulness in RAG Answers](https://example.com/llm-eval-faithfulness)

### What infrastructure choices matter?  ·  confidence 0.65

- **HNSW outperforms IVFFlat on most production workloads up to ~10M vectors, with build parameters m=16 and ef_construction=64 as reasonable defaults.**
